All your devices

Start on the laptop, continue on the phone

  • Same second. Move the music between devices with one tap; it carries on exactly where it was.
  • Your phone is the remote. Control the computer's music from the phone and the other way round.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account. The code is open on GitHub.

Do I need a Spotify or Yandex Music subscription?

No. Segue only reads your playlists and likes from them; the music itself plays from YouTube and SoundCloud.

macOS says it can't open the app

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. Move Segue to Applications, right-click it and choose Open — only the first time.

Android won't install the APK

Allow installing apps from your browser or file manager when Android asks. If an older Segue won't update, remove it once and install the new one.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet: Apple requires signing for every app. It's in the works.

What about my data?

There's no Segue server: your library is stored on your devices, and sync between them is end-to-end encrypted. No analytics, no tracking.
